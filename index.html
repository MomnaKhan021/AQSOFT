FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Talk to us.

Do you sell ready products or build custom software?

Both. The AQ Suite (POS, Commerce, ERP, CRM, HR & Payroll and Insights) is ready to launch and can be customized for you. If you need something entirely new, our team designs and builds it from scratch.

Does AQ POS work without internet?

Yes. AQ POS keeps billing offline and automatically syncs sales and stock once the connection returns.

Can you move my data from my current system?

Yes. We migrate products, customers, stock and history from spreadsheets or your existing software as part of onboarding.

Who owns the code for custom projects?

You do. For custom development you receive full ownership of the source code and your data.

Do you provide support after launch?

Every project includes post-launch support, with ongoing maintenance and monitoring plans available.
